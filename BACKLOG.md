
- **A form at a slot** (okay-desk, 2026-11-06): a screen whose conversation grows wants its forms of opening before
  the conversation and the question's form after it; okay-faces draws every form with its button after the parts,
  and `button.at(slot)` takes buttons of no input only. The desk orders its page by the skin (a flex column, the
  view's groups and the actions' bar dissolved, `order` on the forms and the conversation's blocks). The right word:
  `Button.submit(...).at(slot)` with the form drawn at the slot — okay-faces would need a view of an inline action.
